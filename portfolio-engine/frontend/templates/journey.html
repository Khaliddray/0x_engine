04 // BACKGROUND & DIRECTION

Journey & Foundations

My journey into technology wasn't a straight path. It began with Geography, grew through curiosity about the possibilities of technology, and eventually became a commitment to exploring a new direction for my future.

Milestone 01

Where It Started

2020–2021

I began studying Geography at Nasarawa State University, Keffi (NSUK), around 2020/2021. My academic interests and research focused on environmental science and understanding the relationship between people, places, and their environment.

Over time, I started looking for ways to make my field of study more engaging. Discovering Geographic Information Systems (GIS) introduced me to a different perspective. I saw how technology could transform the way geographical and environmental problems were studied, analyzed, and solved.

That experience planted an important seed: technology could be more than a tool I used in my studies. It could become something I learned to build with.

Milestone 02

Discovering AI and Programming

2023

In 2023, I became fascinated by the incredible potential of artificial intelligence. I started thinking about what I could accomplish if I went beyond using technology and learned how to create it myself.

That curiosity pushed me to begin exploring programming languages and software development. I was taking my first steps into a new field, driven by the possibilities I could see ahead.

However, access to sufficient learning resources and opportunities for consistent hands-on practice became a challenge. I had the interest and willingness to learn, but I needed a better environment to turn that interest into practical experience. I continued hoping for an opportunity that would help bridge that gap.

Milestone 03

A Turning Point

Late 2024

Late 2024 became a significant turning point in my life. I reached a point where I had to reconsider my future and ask myself whether I wanted to continue along the same path or take more deliberate steps toward the life I envisioned.

It was a period of reflection that made the importance of my decisions clearer. I knew I wanted to build something meaningful with my future, and technology remained one of the possibilities I was determined to explore.

Milestone 04

The Learn2Earn Opportunity

2025

Shortly after graduating in 2025, my family introduced me to Learn2Earn. Initially, the opportunity sounded almost too good to be true. Yet, trusting that my family wanted the best for me, I decided to give it a chance.

That decision became an important step in my journey. It offered a pathway to pursue my interest in technology with greater intention and move closer to the practical learning experience I had been seeking.

Looking back, what began as curiosity during my Geography studies gradually developed into a clearer direction. Each stage taught me something about exploration, patience, resilience, and the importance of finding opportunities that turn potential into progress.

Milestone 05

Continuing the Journey

Today

My journey is still unfolding. From discovering GIS to exploring the possibilities of artificial intelligence and programming, each stage has shaped my perspective and strengthened my desire to pursue a future in technology.

I carry with me the lessons of exploration, patience, resilience, and the importance of making the most of the opportunities that come my way.

My goal is to keep learning, embrace new challenges, and use technology to create meaningful opportunities for myself and others.
